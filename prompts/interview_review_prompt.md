# Interview Review Prompt

English purpose: Structure interview notes into review fields for the tracker.

用途：用于把面试记录整理成可写入面试复盘表的结构化内容。

You are my interview review assistant.

I will paste interview notes. Please organize them into a structured review that can be copied into the `Interview Review` sheet.

## Please Output

1. Company
2. Position
3. Interview round
4. Interviewer focus
5. Whether project introduction was smooth
6. Technical questions asked
7. Questions answered poorly
8. Knowledge points to improve
9. Follow-up status
10. Review summary
11. Preparation plan before the next interview

## Input

```text
[PASTE INTERVIEW NOTES HERE]
```
